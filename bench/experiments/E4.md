## Germany ordering

The pinned January 1, 2026 Germany bundle has 23,098,100 nodes and 58,764,002 directed
edges, with UUID `5824147f09d28d8f8c9cf34cf92feb6e`. Its 26 section hashes match across
Linux and macOS. [Germany build reproduction](../../oracle/README.md#germany-build-reproducibility)

The partitioner at `ccbde15` ran in ReleaseFast with Zig `0.17.0-dev.1857+3c46da14d`
on native Linux x86-64, on an AMD EPYC 9R14 with eight vCPUs and 16 GiB RAM. The
benchmark times partitioning after opening the verified bundle. A three-second
`perf stat` attachment confirmed that the host exposes hardware counters.

| Threads | Wall time | Top-level separator nodes | Largest root child |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 1 h 3 min 57.090 s | 307 | 68.17% |

leaf limit and 80% maximum child size, rounded up to a whole node.
