## Leaf paths

`partition.build` now orders each leaf and each separator after the dissection
(#266). Within a range it takes the range's edges in order of input ID gap, keeps an
edge when it joins the ends of two different paths, and writes each path
contiguously from the range's lowest free endpoint. Edges whose input IDs are more
than `max_link_gap` apart are not joined (default 256). Cells, leaf membership and
separator ranks are unchanged, and the pass runs after the dissection, so its output
does not depend on the thread count.

The benchmark reports the dissection alone as `dissection_order_and_tree_xxh3` and
`dissection_adjacent_id_distance`. `order_and_tree_xxh3` and
`partition_adjacent_id_distance` describe the final order, `elapsed_ns` times the
dissection and `leaf_order_ns` the leaf pass. The hashes above are dissection hashes.

The Germany comparison used a bundle built from the same pinned extract without
elevation. It has the same 23,098,100 nodes, 58,764,002 directed edges and Hilbert
ordering hash `299c120d94338f39`, and its eight-thread dissection reproduced
`65694228a79be14e` and the depth table above. Each gap was applied to that same
dissection on an Apple M2 Pro (macOS arm64) in ReleaseFast. The pass took about
2.3 s on one thread. Distances cover all 29,382,001 undirected edges.

| Order | Median | p90 | p99 | Edges at distance 1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hilbert input | 2 | 56 | 4,593 | 11,151,073 (37.95%) |
| Nested dissection | 2 | 56 | 4,734 | 11,162,529 (37.99%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 2 | 2 | 56 | 4,734 | 13,848,733 (47.13%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 4 | 1 | 56 | 4,734 | 15,451,430 (52.59%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 8 | 1 | 57 | 4,734 | 16,547,955 (56.32%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 16 | 1 | 57 | 4,734 | 17,296,799 (58.87%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 64 | 1 | 57 | 4,736 | 18,149,166 (61.77%) |
| Leaf paths, gap 256 | 1 | 56 | 4,736 | 18,551,491 (63.14%) |
| Leaf paths, unlimited gap | 1 | 59 | 5,199 | 18,926,240 (64.41%) |

Gaps 0 and 1 leave the dissection unchanged. Gap 256 keeps the dissection's p90 and
nearly its p99 while placing 63% of edges at consecutive ranks; the unlimited gap
gains 1.3 points more but raises p99 by 10%. The final order-and-tree XXH3 at gap
256 was `611953c2a293977d`.
